The product thesis

Evidence before action.
Automate the repeatable. Reason over the ambiguous. Put non-negotiable guarantees in code. Make uncertainty visible.

The decision

CapabilityDecisionReason
Ticket triageAgentFree-text intent is ambiguous; the next step depends on what the ticket contains.

Customer reply toneSkillStable procedure, variable content.

QA sampling / arithmeticNeitherReproducibility and exact calculations belong in code.

Why this is different

Decision Receipt

Each high-impact decision carries evidence, decision source, risk and next action instead of an opaque answer.

Abstention is a state

The system can say “insufficient evidence” rather than forcing a confident but unsafe output.

Trust Test Suite

Tests include cases where the correct behavior is not to act: do not invent a refund, do not miss an escalation, do not force a weak bug match.

Falsifiable metrics

Success is safe automation: missed critical escalations, policy violations, misroutes, useful insights and human overrides.
